What saving does

Off by default. Turn it on before you type personal details if you want this case here after you close the app. On an iPhone, leaving this page for a while (another app, a locked screen) can make it reload, and with saving off a reload clears the case. A saved case holds your name, address and items, plus any photos or PDFs you add under Evidence, in this browser with no password, so anyone who opens this page on this device can see them. A drawn signature is never saved.

About, privacy and sources

What this is. Dispute Desk drafts letters from the facts you enter, following the federal baseline in Frenny Credit Notes (FCRA, FDCPA, Regulation V). It isn't a law firm and doesn't give legal advice. It doesn't mail, file or contact anyone for you. State law can add protections or change deadlines. If you do this for clients as a business, check the credit repair rules, federal and your state's, with a lawyer.

Your data. Nothing about your case is saved unless you turn on "Save this case on this device". When it is on, your work, and any photos or PDFs you add under Evidence, are kept in this browser on this device so they are here when you come back, and inside Claude a private copy of the case (not the files) is also kept in your Claude account for this page. A drawn signature is never saved. Small settings, like your light or dark choice, are remembered either way. Text and screenshots go to Claude only when you press "Read it for me", and that works only inside Claude. "Clear everything" erases the case, the files and every saved copy.

Addresses and dates. Every bureau address in the letters is built in: each bureau's dispute address, and its fraud and identity theft address for the block letter. They are as of this month, from each bureau's own pages and the CFPB. Check each bureau's own site before you mail. A creditor or collector in the built-in list (the names that come up as you type its name) also has its address filled in, read from the company's own website or forms and checked this month. A company that is not in the list asks for its address. Check the address on your statement or notice before you mail. The breach list is as of this month and is not complete.
